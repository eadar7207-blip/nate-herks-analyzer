# Nate Herk Analysis — August 21, 2026

## [Practice Any Hard Conversation Before It Matters](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/lM3KWEmBdMg)
*August 18 at 03:00 PM*

WHAT IT'S ABOUT

Nate made this short video to point out a problem with how most workplace training works: we watch videos and go to workshops, but never actually practice the hard conversations before we have them for real. He's showing off a new tool from a company called Synthesia (a company that makes AI-generated videos with realistic digital people) that lets you rehearse tough talks — like sales calls — with an AI character that acts like a real person and gives you feedback.

THE KEY POINTS

- **Watching a training video doesn't make you good at something — you have to actually practice it.** Nate compares it to watching a boxing unboxing video and then stepping into the ring, which nobody would do.
- Most companies train people the wrong way, expecting workers to "magically" improve just from watching content and attending sessions.
- **Synthesia's new "role-play sessions" tool lets you practice a full conversation with an AI avatar (a computer-generated character that looks and talks like a person) that pushes back on you like a real prospect would.**
- The AI coaches you in real time — it tells you where you messed up and lets you try again to improve.
- This can be used for many real situations: sales calls, handling objections (when a customer gives you reasons not to buy), customer support, and leadership talks.
- **The future of training is practicing the things that matter, not just watching more videos.**

THE METHOD OR FRAMEWORK

The tool works in a simple loop:
1. **Pick a scenario** — for example, an important sales call you have tomorrow.
2. **Practice with the AI avatar** — you have the actual conversation out loud, and the AI responds realistically and challenges you, just like a real person would.
3. **Get coached in real time** — the AI points out exactly where you went wrong.
4. **Try again** — you redo the conversation and improve with each attempt until you feel ready for the real thing.

HOW THIS APPLIES TO AI REAL ESTATE

This is directly useful if you're building an AI real estate consulting business, because real estate runs on high-stakes conversations — pitching sellers, handling nervous buyers, and pushing back on lowball offers. Imagine one of your agent clients has a listing appointment where they need to convince a homeowner to price their house realistically. Instead of just hoping the agent knows what to say, you could set them up with a role-play tool where an AI plays a stubborn homeowner who insists their house is worth more than it is. The agent practices the pitch, gets coached on their weak spots, and walks into the real appointment already sharp. You could even sell this practice setup as part of your consulting package — "we don't just give your agents scripts, we train them to actually deliver them."
